Romero Britto

1 work in the collection

Romero Britto is a Brazilian neo-pop artist whose work blends cubism, pop art, and graffiti into vibrant, instantly recognizable imagery. Based between Miami and his native Brazil, Britto's colorful, optimistic style has made him one of the best-known contemporary pop artists internationally.
