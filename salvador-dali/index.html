Salvador Dali

2 works in the collection

Salvador Dalí (1904–1989) was a Spanish surrealist painter and one of the most influential artists of the 20th century, celebrated for his precise draftsmanship, dreamlike imagery, and flamboyant public persona. Beyond his paintings, Dalí produced an extensive body of prints and sculpture that remain widely collected today.
