David Najar

2 works in the collection

David Najar is a contemporary painter known for richly textured, palette-knife cityscapes and abstract works built from thick layers of color. His paintings favor bold contrast and physical texture over fine detail, giving his cities and figures a sculptural, almost tactile quality.
