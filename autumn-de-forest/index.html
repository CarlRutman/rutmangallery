Artist: Autumn de Forest

Autumn de Forest has been cited as the only living artist with work in the Louvre, and delivered a piece she painted live at age 9 that helped launch her national recognition.

Biography & Style:

Autumn de Forest is a global phenomenon who has been painting professionally since the age of five. Described by the Discovery Channel as a “child genius,” Autumn has transcended the label of “prodigy” to become one of the most successful and collected young artists of the 21st century.

Her work is a joyous exploration of art history, acting as a bridge between the classic and the contemporary. Autumn fearlessly experiments with techniques inspired by the masters—channeling the pop art of Andy Warhol, the surrealism of Salvador Dalí, and the dripped abstraction of Jackson Pollock—while injecting them with her own youthful optimism and vibrant color palette. She is not just mimicking history; she is remixing it for a new generation.

Beyond the canvas, Autumn is a dedicated humanitarian. She was the youngest artist ever appointed to the President’s Committee on the Arts and the Humanities and has been honored by the Vatican for her charitable work. When you collect an Autumn de Forest, you are not just buying a painting; you are investing in a career that is already historic and a spirit that is dedicated to changing the world.

Key Elements of Her Work:

  • Signature Style: Pop-Surrealism / Abstract Expressionism / “Pop-Ab”.
  • The Aesthetic: Bright, bold colors, playful imagery, and large-scale experimentation.
  • Recurring Themes: Hearts, American flags, Barbie imagery, homages to Grant Wood (“American Graphic”), and dripping abstraction.
  • The “Pull”: Her unique “pull painting” technique, where she drags paint across the canvas to create self-portrait silhouettes and abstract horizons.
