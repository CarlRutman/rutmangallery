Jim Warren

1 work in the collection

Jim Warren is an American fantasy and pop-surrealist painter whose hyper-realistic, imaginative scenes blend classic Americana with dreamlike and fantastical elements. He built an extensive early career painting book and album covers before turning to fine art.
