Linda Le Kinff

1 work in the collection

Linda Le Kinff is a French contemporary artist whose portraits combine modern figuration with the flattened perspective and gold-leaf ornamentation of Byzantine iconography. Her work is known for its rich color, decorative pattern, and elegant, stylized female subjects.
