Duaiv

1 work in the collection

Duaiv (Marc-Antoine Coulon) is a French-born impressionist painter celebrated for vivid floral still lifes and sun-drenched Mediterranean and Provençal landscapes. His loose, expressive brushwork and saturated color palette draw on the tradition of French impressionism.
