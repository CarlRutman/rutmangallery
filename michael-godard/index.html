Michael Godard

7 works in the collection

Michael Godard is an American pop-surrealist painter based in Las Vegas, best known for his mischievous martini-olive characters and neon-lit casino and bar-culture scenes. His bold, graphic style blends humor with technical precision, and his work has become closely associated with Las Vegas nightlife and pop art collecting.
