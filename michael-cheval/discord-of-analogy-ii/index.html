Discord of Analogy II

by Michael Cheval

Description:

In “Discord of Analogy,” Michael Cheval presents a stunning visual puzzle that challenges our understanding of gravity, communication, and perspective. A quintessential example of his Absurdist style, this painting depicts two musicians occupying the same room but living in completely different worlds.

The composition is split between two opposing gravitational forces. On the “floor,” a composer dressed in green and purple Renaissance attire sits in contemplation, seemingly waiting for inspiration. Above him, defying physics, a woman in a vibrant red dress plays a grand piano that is bolted to the “ceiling”.

The genius of the piece lies in the connection between them. Sheets of music fall from her piano, tumbling “down” through the air to land at the man’s feet. This suggests that while they are separated by their perspectives, they are connected by the art they create. The title “Discord of Analogy” hints at the struggle to find harmony when two people see the world from opposite angles—a visual metaphor for a relationship where both partners are present, but grounded in different realities.
