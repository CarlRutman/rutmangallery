Itzchak Tarkay

1 work in the collection

Itzchak Tarkay (1935–2012) was an Israeli painter renowned for his elegant, patterned depictions of women in cafés and gardens. His decorative use of color and pattern, influenced by Matisse and the Nabis, made him one of the most widely collected artists of his generation.
