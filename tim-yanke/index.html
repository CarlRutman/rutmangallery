Tim Yanke

2 works in the collection

Tim Yanke is an American contemporary artist known for a bold, graphic style applied to sports, wildlife, and Americana subjects. He has served as an official artist for multiple professional sports leagues and teams, and his work is recognized for its high-contrast color and energetic composition.
